1 · The Locked Spec (what we are building)

Cognitive job: Given a schema of typed quantities and a snapshot of typed observations, decide, for the user's question and for every claim a drafter makes, what the evidence entails about it — one closed Entailment — and say no more than that. See .genesis/PLAN.md §2–§5 for the full claim and the closed vocabulary.

Inputs: (schema, observations, question, draft)  →  Outputs: a Brief of Entailments (entailed · weakened · contradicted · ungrounded · conflicted), each carrying its support, its cut, its open ends or its core, or a typed fault; never a score, never prose first.

Autonomy level: Fully autonomous end to end; the drafter is untrusted input and is a pure function; there is no human in the loop and no model in the repo.

Failure tolerance: Fail toward the weaker statement — when the evidence is silent say nothing about the quantity, when it conflicts refuse the tree, when input is unreadable return a typed fault.

Filled during Genesis (G0–G2, .genesis/PLAN.md, §16 handoff to G3), following the shape used in the sibling projects' own .genesis/DONE.html — content is project-specific, structure only is borrowed. No engine code exists yet: M1 (contracts) is a separate milestone with its own PR and its own verifier.
